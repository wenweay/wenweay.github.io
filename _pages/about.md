I am an MSc student in Biomedical Engineering at [Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it/) (School of Industrial and Information Engineering). I received my B.E. in Biomedical Engineering from the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), Shenzhen, China, in 2023, where I was a member of the Neural Computing and Control Laboratory (NCCLab) under the supervision of Dr. [Quanying Liu](https://faculty.sustech.edu.cn/?tagid=liuqy&iscss=1&snapid=1&orderby=date&go=1&lang=en).

My research focuses on **non-invasive brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)** and **machine learning for neural signals**. My long-term goal is to use artificial intelligence to build practical, general-purpose brain-computer interfaces.

Current research
------
For my Master's thesis I am developing **RI-JEPA**, a self-supervised Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture for decoding Chinese imagined speech from non-invasive EEG. It uses the naturally paired *reading-phase* and *imagined-phase* EEG of the same sentence as a cross-condition training signal, and is evaluated on the Chisco dataset (OpenNeuro ds005170) against MAE, contrastive, and single-modality JEPA baselines.

Research interests
------
* Brain-computer interfaces (motor imagery, SSVEP, imagined speech)
* Self-supervised and representation learning for EEG
* Biomedical signal processing and medical image analysis
