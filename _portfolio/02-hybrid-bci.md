**Individual project, NCCLab, SUSTech (Mar. 2022 – Oct. 2022)**

Motor imagery (MI) suffers from limited decoding accuracy and few degrees of freedom, while SSVEP requires users to fixate on flickering stimuli for a long time, which causes visual fatigue. We proposed a deep-learning hybrid BCI that combines MI and SSVEP and can decode single-paradigm as well as multi-paradigm inputs.

![Two-stream CNN architecture](/images/TSCNN.png)

* **Method**: a two-stream convolutional network, with one stream for MI inputs and the other for SSVEP inputs.
* **Results**: classification accuracy of 96% for the hybrid paradigm, 94% for SSVEP, and 70% for MI.
* **Publication**: Luo, W., Yin, W., Liu, Q., & Qu, Y. (2023). *Brain-Apparatus Communication: A Journal of Bacomics*, 2(1), 2258938. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/27706710.2023.2258938)
