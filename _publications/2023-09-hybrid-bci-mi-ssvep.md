Motor imagery (MI) has limited decoding accuracy and few degrees of freedom, while SSVEP requires users to fixate on flickering stimuli for a long time, which easily causes visual fatigue. We proposed a hybrid brain-computer interface based on deep learning that combines MI and SSVEP. The model uses a two-stream convolutional network, with one stream for MI inputs and one for SSVEP inputs, and can decode single-paradigm as well as hybrid inputs.

**Results**: classification accuracy of 96% for the hybrid paradigm, 94% for SSVEP, and 70% for MI.
